How to play

  1. Press Start. The pads light up in a pattern, one by one.
  2. Repeat the pattern by tapping the pads in the same order.
  3. Each round adds one more step, and the pattern gets faster.
  4. Keys 1-4 also work. In Strict mode, one mistake ends the game.
